Show the full App Store description

Made for iPhone Duo: the hinge is the fold. Pile on the fillings, close the phone to fold the tortilla (slam it and a crunchy shell cracks), slide it into its holder on the cover screen, then open up for three tough judges. Taco Fold is a cozy taco-building game with real food physics, and it plays on every iPhone: turn it sideways and drag the fold tab. FOLD IT WITH YOUR PHONE On iPhone Duo, close the phone to fold the taco: the hinge is the fold. Overstuff it and the filling squirts out the ends. Slam it shut and a crunchy shell cracks. On any other iPhone, turn it sideways and drag the fold tab. BUILD IT Your tortilla sits on a painted plate with sixteen bowls around it. Drag a handful from any bowl and drop it where you want it. Salsas drizzle from the spoon as you drag. Push food around with your fingertip to even it out. Pick from 93 ingredients and 16 tortillas, from carne asada, al pastor and birria to nopales, squash blossoms and sixteen salsas, wrapped in corn, flour, a hard shell, a cheese shell or even a cactus paddle. REAL FOOD PHYSICS Every piece has weight and friction. Toppings are drawn to true size, piles slump, sauce makes things stick, and runny salsas creep when you tilt. Give the phone a shove and the toppings fly. Turn it upside down and whatever isn't glued down falls off. PLATE IT AND FACE THE JUDGES Slide the folded taco into its holder on the cover screen, then open up for scorecards from Abuela, Chef Nacho and Brie. They score portions, how evenly the filling runs along the fold, flavor balance, looks, structure and plating. Find 33 named combos, from al pastor to quesabirria to a K-Town taco, and avoid crimes like cheese on fish. ORDER UP! Customers slide you tickets. Match the order, nail the spice level and beat the clock. Play Today's Special, one ticket a day, the same for everyone. Race through three orders in Food Truck Rush, or pass the phone for a two-player Taco-Off with the same ticket. A TOY ON THE COVER Shut the phone between tacos and the cover screen becomes a toy. Tap to toss toppings that tumble with the phone's real tilt, shake to send them flying, poke Taquito the taco for kitchen wisdom, or play Taco Catch, a 30-second topping rain. KEEP YOUR BEST Name your best tacos, keep the top 50 in the Hall of Fame, share a card of your favourite, and earn badges. Día de Muertos and Las Posadas bring their own specials. VoiceOver can build, plate and play. Everything stays on your device: no accounts, no ads, no tracking.
