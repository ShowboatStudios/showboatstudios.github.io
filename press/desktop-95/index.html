Made for iPhone Duo. Fold it like a laptop: the top screen is a mid-90s monitor and the bottom half becomes a clicky beige keyboard with a working trackpad (and a coffee mug you can spill). Close the lid and the computer stands by while the cover screen keeps the clock; open it and you are right where you left off. Plays on every iPhone too. Desktop 95 is a whole pocket-sized beige computer, with pinball, card games, paint and a pretend web, drawn pixel by pixel and made entirely from scratch. BUILT FOR THE FOLD - Laptop pose: the hinge is the lid, the bottom screen is the keyboard deck and trackpad - Open flat: the whole inner screen is the monitor - Shut: the cover screen becomes a tiny system tray with the clock, what is playing and what your programs are up to - Music keeps playing with the lid closed - Choose whether closing the lid stands by or shuts down On any other iPhone, hold it sideways for a full landscape desktop, or upright for a tall one. WHAT YOU CAN DO - Play Pinball on an original space-trooper table with missions, ranks and multiball - Clear boards in Mines, Solitaire and FreeCell - Munch your way through Number Nosh, a quick maths puzzle - Head north in Dawson Bound, a Klondike gold rush journey where nobody gets hurt - Draw in Paint with pencil, brush, airbrush, fill, shapes and a 28-colour palette, then set it as wallpaper or save it to your Photos - Write in Jotter, work things out in Calculator and tidy your files in folders - Type commands at the command line: DIR, CD, TYPE, HELP and more - Browse the Web Surfer's pretend retro pages, complete with hit counter and guestbook - Play five original songs on the music player, even with the lid shut - Watch the screen savers, and find the crash screen if you dare A REAL LITTLE COMPUTER Little windows you can drag, resize, minimise and maximise. A Start menu with nested submenus. A taskbar with a clock. Long-press for right-click menus. A pretend disk that remembers your files, a trash can that holds your mistakes, and a Setup window for wallpapers, colour schemes and sounds. Shut it down properly when you are done. THE DESKTOP 95 KEYBOARD A two-tone mechanical-style keyboard with pixel legends, a Caps Lock light, key repeat and a satisfying click. Prefer the iPhone keyboard? One switch in Setup. ORIGINAL FROM TOP TO BOTTOM Every icon, cursor, wallpaper and sound is made for this app. Nothing is copied from any real operating system. No ads. No accounts. No tracking. Your files stay on your phone.
