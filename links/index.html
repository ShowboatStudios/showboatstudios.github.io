Campaign links

One App Store link per app per channel, in Apple's campaign format https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id<ID>?pt=<PROVIDER_TOKEN>&ct=<campaign>&mt=8. Downloads show up per campaign in App Store Connect > Analytics > Acquisition. Channels come from config.campaigns in /data/apps.json.

PROVIDER_TOKEN is empty in /data/apps.json, so these are plain store links. Paste the token (App Store Connect > Analytics > Acquisition > Campaigns > Generate Campaign Link, the pt= value) and every link below becomes a tracked campaign link.
